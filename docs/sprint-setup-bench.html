Staff Engineer

Decide what "done" means before anyone starts building

Staff Engineer runs AI agents through checkpoints they can't skip, and refuses to close work without proof. None of that helps if the goal is fuzzy — agents will happily declare a fuzzy goal met. So the system won't start until a person has done the part no agent can do: said what will be true when it's finished, and how anyone could check that it's true. This page walks you through that, then hands you the exact words to give your agent. You don't write code here, and you don't need to read any.

OneThe workexample

What are you building, and what will be true when it's finished?

One piece of work, one finish line. If it takes two sentences to describe, it's two pieces of work — do the first one.

TwoThe check

How would you check it yourself?

Not how a computer would check it — how you would, if you had to satisfy yourself it was really done. Everything else follows from this answer.

"If you cannot write the command, you do not yet know what you are building. Stop and define the goal before planning." Staff Engineer, on the stopping condition

That's the system's rule, and it sounds like it's asking you to write code. It isn't. It's asking whether you can describe a check precisely enough that there's nothing left to argue about. Do that here, in your own words, and your agent turns it into the command.

If you were checking this by hand, what would you actually do?
ThreeThe pieces

What has to be built, and in what order?

Your agent will come back with a plan and ask you to approve it. Approving a plan you have no opinion about is rubber-stamping. Sketch your own expectation first — rough is fine, it's a yardstick, not an instruction.

The shape of it

It has to come to a point. Work flows one way — nothing can wait on something that's waiting on it. And every line has to end at the same last piece: the one that, once finished, makes your check pass. Two separate endings means you've described two pieces of work, not one.

FourWhat to say next

Where you stand

The system refuses to move on missing pieces rather than guessing at them. Same here — this is that refusal, moved earlier, where it's cheap. Each numbered line below is something to say to an agent that has this repository open — not a button on this page.
